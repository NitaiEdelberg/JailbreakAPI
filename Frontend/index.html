Catch jailbreaks before they reach your model.

How it works

  1. 1

    Normalize

    Leetspeak, Unicode homoglyphs and letter-spacing are folded back into plain words, so obfuscation does not slip past.

  2. 2

    Rule layer

    Curated override idioms plus compositional rules (a disclosure verb near a secret noun). Built for precision.

  3. 3

    ML classifier

    TF-IDF + logistic regression with calibrated probabilities and a threshold chosen to keep false positives under 2%.

  4. 4

    Verdict

    Safe, suspicious or malicious, with the highest scanner risk and the matched phrase surfaced for review.
